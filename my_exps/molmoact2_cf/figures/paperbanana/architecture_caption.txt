Architecture of LoRA action-expert RL on a frozen π0.5 specialist. Three BEHAVIOR-1K cameras and a language prompt enter a frozen PaliGemma VLM. An encoder–decoder bottleneck yields an RL token z used only by the critic. The policy is LoRA on the frozen gemma_300m action expert: a 10-step Euler unroll with lora_scale=1 produces the executed chunk a, while a stop-grad unroll with frozen weights produces the reference ã. A 10-head chunk critic Q(s, flatten(a)) bootstraps from EMA LoRA. Ice-blue modules are frozen; coral modules are trained.
