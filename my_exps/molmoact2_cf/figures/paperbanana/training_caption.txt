Training protocol. Stage 0 fills replay with 100 specialist rollouts (9650 decision chunks). Stage 1 trains only the token autoencoder (8000 steps). Stage 2 trains the critic with TD while LoRA is held to the specialist by BC and an L2 anchor (8000 steps). After a 1-episode probe, Stage 3 runs 50 online rounds: publish EMA LoRA, collect one episode on instance 308, then 5×n_chunks off-policy updates. The −Q actor term is gated off until Stage 3; the anchor and BC terms stay on throughout.
